# Apple Watch, Apple Health and watch-app features Tensile could add

Research round, 5 November 2026. Question from the user (Apple Watch Ultra, AirPods Pro 3 for heart rate, Cronometer for food): what health and fitness features could the app add, especially with an Apple Watch, and what else could connect to Apple Health (mindfulness, how I'm feeling, and so on).

**How this was researched.** The existing docs (`roadmap.md`, `competitors.md`, `health-metrics.md`, `app-audit.md`) and the code (`App/Services/HealthKitClient*.swift`, `VitalsModel.swift`, `CheckInStore.swift`, `Watch/`, `WatchWidgets/`, `Packages/AppCore`) were read first so nothing below proposes what already exists. HealthKit facts were checked against Apple's reference pages on 5 Nov 2026 (the `.md` variants of developer.apple.com pages are fetchable from the sandbox; the HTML pages are not). Evidence was checked by web search; PubMed itself is blocked from the sandbox, so papers were confirmed through PMC, publisher or university pages. Anything not confirmed is marked **(uncertain)**.

**Evidence labels**: *strong* = meta-analyses/RCTs or guideline consensus; *moderate* = large cohorts or small RCT pools; *weak* = small or indirect studies; *none* = usability or plumbing only. **Build size**: S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = multi-week.

**What already exists** (so it is not re-proposed): strain/recovery/sleep scores with HRV baseline; sleep stages, debt and regularity; weekly plan with if-then, consistency, training blocks, deloads (`DeloadAdvisor`), e1RM and plateau detection (`TrainingTools.StrengthProgress`), muscle map; lifting Watch app with per-set RPE, rest timer with end haptic, Watch-owned `HKWorkout`; complications (recovery+strain, next session); iPhone Live Activity for the live workout; merged Watch/AirPods/Garmin heart rate; HRR after workouts (own calculation plus Apple's `heartRateRecoveryOneMinute`); walking HR, SpO2, respiratory rate, wrist temperature (in the illness watch), time in daylight, sleep-apnoea events, mindful minutes, caffeine, alcohol and water read from Health; State of Mind (daily mood), mindful sessions, workout effort score and estimated meals written to Health; camera HRV; STOP-Bang; body age; heat and weather advice; cycle note (`CycleEnergy`, from the check-in's "On my period").

### Should wrist temperature carry more weight in the illness watch?

Yes, modestly. Continuous wearable skin temperature with a personal baseline is one of the better early-infection signals: the TemPredict ring study reached 82% sensitivity and 63% specificity over a 21-day window using temperature plus heart and breathing rate [B9]; a systematic review found heart rate the most consistent signal, then skin temperature, then respiratory rate, with peripheral temperature more sensitive than oral [B10]; a bracelet study kept sensitivity under 80% [B11]. Single-threshold wrist thermometers do badly [B12]. So the current design (temperature as one of several signals, two-day rule, luteal allowance) is right; the change worth making is to use a z-score against the 14-night SD rather than a fixed +0.5 °C, as the other signals already do, and to add `appleSleepingBreathingDisturbances` as a fourth signal. Evidence **moderate**; size **S**.

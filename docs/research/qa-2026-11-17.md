# User-perspective QA, 17 November 2026

Four audits traced what a daily user would see (the app can't run in the build sandbox, so each finding was followed through the code from the screen to the number), and one ran five realistic people through every calculator. About 65 faults were fixed. None of the fixes has been run on a phone: AppCore changes are unit-tested (1,177 tests), app changes are compiled by CI.

## Recovery

**Was:** the 7-day HRV trend put the score in a band (34-66 on most days) and last night moved it inside the band. Over a simulated year 78% of days fell in 34-66 and 7% read "Recovered"; a small move in the average could drop the score 30 points.

**Now:** a smooth blend of four readings against your own 30 nights: 7-day HRV 35%, last night's HRV 20%, sleeping heart rate 25%, sleep score 20%. A usual night scores about 57. The trial rule stays as the label: a 7-day HRV average below your normal range is an easy day (Plews 2012, 2013; Javaloyes 2019). Ring and watch HRV are put on one scale before use. The recovery sheet shows the method.

## Today and Health (15 findings)

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| One bad night became a "major" two-day illness alert at midnight | The day before moves back while the reading is still yesterday's |
| Sleep ring blank from 00:00; tiles showed yesterday's values unlabelled | The night before stays, labelled "Yesterday" |
| Health showed an evening HRV sample dated tomorrow | Nights that haven't happened are dropped |
| Calorie target climbed 500-1,000 kcal through the day | A usual full day's burn until maintenance is measured |
| A ring fragment replaced the watch's full night | The ring is used when it covers 70% of the other tracker's night |
| Breathing rate fell back to one old sample | The night's average or nothing |
| Old readings counted as today's "to watch" | Only current readings count; temperature flags need 5 nights |
| °C on the illness sign for Fahrenheit users; mislabelled temperature history | One unit setting everywhere |
| Nights under 2 h dropped from sleep debt | Counted from 1 h |
| "0 g" for a nutrient the food app didn't send | "--" |
| Zones and strain used sleeping heart rate as resting | Apple Health's awake resting heart rate |

Not fixed: after a long flight, bed-by and caffeine times stay on home time for a week or two (needs the time zone stored with each night).

## Workouts (14 findings)

Each set is judged against the goal it had when done; swapping an exercise shows the new lift's plan; rating in the set editor moves the next goal; a changed weight carries to the sets still to come; supersets point at the right set after a ramp set; finishing with nothing logged offers Discard; the weekly review keeps the cardio plan; a missed session placed first is explained and the next week returns to the standing order; planks read in seconds; bodyweight lifts read "Bodyweight × 8"; "RPE" wording is gone from the Watch, history and notes; Watch pause freezes both clocks.

Partly fixed: a paused session's saved duration (and so its strain) still includes the pause; the minimised workout bar and Lock Screen still show wall time.

## Reminders, medications, settings (20 findings)

No notification permission prompt at launch; stretch reminder off until chosen; workout reminders skip days already trained and rest mode; the weigh-in line is weekly; snoozes stop after Taken; the Lock Screen doses card has an off switch, stays away once dismissed, shows a dose from two hours before its time and not past midnight; a switch for measurement reminders; the Monday review isn't sent once opened; forms no longer lose edits; a drink doesn't mark the evening check-in done; changing a dose time keeps today's taken state; "monohydrate" isn't read as creatine; Oura sync doesn't hold up the scores.
